    <h1>Commercial Support for Quarkus</h1>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="component-wrapper options-band">
  <div class="width-12-12">
    <p>Quarkus is an open source project maintained by a vibrant community.
    For organizations that need guaranteed support levels, long-term maintenance,
    or coverage for end-of-life versions, commercial support is available from
    several vendors.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="component-wrapper options-band">
  <div class="width-12-12">
    <h2>About the Commonhaus Open Source Support Initiative</h2>
    <p>Quarkus is a member of the <a href="https://www.commonhaus.org/" target="blank">Commonhaus Foundation</a>,
    which runs the <a href="https://www.commonhaus.org/about/ossi.html" target="blank">Open Source Support Initiative (OSSI)</a>
    to help connect organizations with qualified commercial support providers.</p>
    <p>OSSI partners are vetted by the Foundation and must meet requirements around
    security practices, CVE remediation experience, and responsible disclosure.
    Participation in the initiative does not grant governance rights or authority
    over the Quarkus project roadmap.</p>
    <p>Learn more at <a href="https://www.commonhaus.org/about/ossi.html" target="blank">commonhaus.org/about/ossi</a>.</p>
  </div>
</div>
